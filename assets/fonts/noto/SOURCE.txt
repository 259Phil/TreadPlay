Noto Sans Symbols and Noto Sans Symbols 2 — glyph subsets for Flutter Web.

Purpose: the app uses a few symbols that Inter/Roboto do not contain
(→ ← ↔ in legal and l10n strings; ✓ ▸ ✦ ✧ on Wanderweg, run, Schmiede-Baum
and Album). Without a bundled fallback, Flutter Web downloads those Noto
families from fonts.gstatic.com. These subsets close that gap.

Upstream (official Noto Project, SIL OFL 1.1):
  https://github.com/notofonts/symbols
  NotoSansSymbols-v2.003  (hinted/ttf/NotoSansSymbols-Regular.ttf)
  NotoSansSymbols2-v2.008 (hinted/ttf/NotoSansSymbols2-Regular.ttf)

Subset with fonttools pyftsubset:
  NotoSansSymbols-Regular-subset.ttf   U+2190 U+2192 U+2194
  NotoSansSymbols2-Regular-subset.ttf  U+25B8 U+2713 U+2726 U+2727

Licence files: OFL-NotoSansSymbols.txt, OFL-NotoSansSymbols2.txt.
Family names kept as upstream ("Noto Sans Symbols" / "Noto Sans Symbols 2").
